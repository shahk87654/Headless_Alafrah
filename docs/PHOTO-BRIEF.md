# Photography brief

## Every product

- Hero view: the complete piece on a neutral floor, no stock-style staging.
- 45-degree view: edges, base, and scale visible.
- Top-down view: surface and slab pattern.
- Edge close-up: thickness, finish, and cut edge.
- In-room view: the piece in a real UAE residential or commercial setting.
- Scale reference: one hand or one everyday object beside the product.

## Every material

- Macro texture at 3000px minimum.
- Full-slab photograph with natural edges if available.
- Bookmatched pair for the home hero.
- Honed and polished surfaces shown separately.

Use a clean colour-balanced image workflow. Store one dominant colour in the product metadata as a flat fallback. All image crops must preserve veins and edges; avoid decorative overlays.

## Collection and home

- Collection banner: 3:2 landscape photograph, title placed beside the image.
- Home hero bookmatched pair: 6000px wide in total, with a visible seam for mirroring.
- Shop-the-look room: 16:9 room image with precise pin coordinates.
